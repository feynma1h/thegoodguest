# 0343 — day and night are always on the room page

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Status:** Decided

## Context

0312 said the control would move to the product room page if it drew twins.

## What we chose

The existing ☀ day / ☾ night control (a button in the viewer, bottom right) is
now what the product shows whenever twins are drawn; it was checked on the real
room page. The room opens in day.

## Why

It already worked there; no second control.

## What would change this decision

A finished room with no twins has no control (the lighting rig is built from the
twins), and the dark night background sits behind the page's own dark-on-light
header text, which loses contrast at night. Both are open.
